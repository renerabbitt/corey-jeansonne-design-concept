Manrope lends the headlines an open, architectural character. DM Sans keeps navigation and body text clear. Small uppercase captions, fine rules, generous spacing, and restrained drafting references form a consistent visual language.

Motion with purpose.

Gentle reveals and subtle fades accompany scrolling. Image zoom and an interactive portfolio invite closer inspection. Reduced-motion preferences are respected, and the layout adapts from wide desktop screens to narrow phones and foldable sizes.
